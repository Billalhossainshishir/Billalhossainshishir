# RAMIFY OS — University Industry Project

## Overview

RAMIFY OS is a browser-based AI product-trust demonstration developed as a University of Tasmania industry project. The system is designed to produce auditable decision receipts from structured product evidence and support mandatory human-review workflows.

## My role

**Project Manager**

My responsibilities include:

- Project planning and coordination
- Requirements and scope management
- Team responsibility coordination
- Functional testing
- Documentation
- Client feedback tracking
- Architecture and user-guidance support
- Scripted demonstration and final-presentation preparation
